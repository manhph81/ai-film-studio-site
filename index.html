AI Film Studio

A personal, self-hosted tool for producing and publishing short videos.

AI Film Studio is software that runs on its owner's own computer. It helps the owner produce short vertical videos (research, script, voice-over, images, editing) and, when the owner chooses, upload those videos to the owner's own accounts on YouTube, Facebook (a Facebook Page the owner manages) and TikTok.

What it does with your accounts

  • Uploads videos that the owner produced and approved, with the title, description and hashtags the owner chose.
  • Schedules those uploads and, on YouTube, adds them to a playlist for a video series.
  • Reads the status of those uploads (processing, published) and the link of each post.

It does not read messages, comments, followers, analytics or any other data, and it is not offered to other users. There is no server operated by us: everything runs locally on the owner's machine.
