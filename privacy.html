Privacy Policy

AI Film Studio · Effective date: 24 November 2026

AI Film Studio ("the app") is personal software that runs only on its owner's own computer. It is used by its owner to publish videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page and TikTok account. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

1. Data the app accesses

  • Account authorization. When the owner connects an account, the platform (Google / YouTube, Meta / Facebook, TikTok) returns access and refresh tokens. The app uses them only to act on the owner's behalf.
  • Basic account information needed to show which account is connected: channel name, Page name, TikTok nickname, and the identifiers the platform returns.
  • Information about the app's own uploads: video IDs, links, processing and publishing status, scheduled time, playlist ID.

The app does not access messages, comments, followers, contacts, analytics, payment information or any data of other users.

2. How the data is used

Only to upload videos that the owner produced and approved, set their title, description, privacy and schedule, add them to the owner's playlists, and show the owner the result. The data is not used for advertising, profiling, training machine-learning models, or any other purpose.

3. Where the data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Tokens are stored in a local configuration file readable only by the owner's user account; upload records are stored in the project folder on the same computer. The app has no server, no cloud database and no analytics or tracking.

4. Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone. It is sent only to the platform it belongs to (Google / YouTube, Meta / Facebook, TikTok) in the API requests needed to publish the owner's videos.

5. Google API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube account you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. AI Film Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account in the app, which deletes them from the computer. Upload records are kept with the video project and are deleted when the project is deleted. Step-by-step instructions: Data Deletion Instructions. You can also revoke the app's access at any time:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites)
  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and is used only by its adult owner.

8. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: archiepham81@gmail.com

  • Dùng để: đăng, hẹn giờ, đưa vào danh sách phát những video chủ studio đã duyệt. Không dùng cho quảng cáo, phân tích hành vi hay huấn luyện AI.
  • Lưu ở đâu: chỉ trên máy của chủ studio (file cấu hình chỉ chủ máy đọc được). Không có máy chủ, không có theo dõi.
